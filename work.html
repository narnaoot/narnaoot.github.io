Building iCloud's Outage Tracking from Scratch

iCloud launched to 300 million users with no system for tracking outages across its services. I built the whole thing — incident reporting, root cause identification, live dashboards, and the process that made it all run.

Incident Management Dashboard Design Process Creation Business Intelligence
01Define the problem nobody owned
Multiple services, no shared incident tracking, no root cause process.
02Build the system
Incident reporting, root cause analysis, database, live Tableau dashboards.
03Harden the backbone
Drove disaster recovery: strategy, extensive testing, documentation, architecture improvements to keep iCloud's operating system and backbone healthy.
04Guide strategy
Data showed where new features vs. technical debt needed balance. Leadership could finally see the tradeoffs.
From zero visibility to a system that guided resource allocation across iCloud's engineering org.

New workflow across 40+ engineering groups. Goals, success metrics, real ownership.
03Wire up the intelligence
Automation plus a live dashboard. Real-time visibility into something that used to be a black box.
Kernel updates stop being a source of surprise. Leadership gets the signal. Engineers get their time back.
