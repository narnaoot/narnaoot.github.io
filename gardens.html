Personal Project · Visual Storytelling · San Francisco

Mapping the secret gardens
of San Francisco

Years ago, when I worked downtown, a coworker led me into a Wells Fargo, past a security guard, onto an elevator, and up to a rooftop garden — a hidden public space open to anyone who knows it's there. I've loved secrets like that ever since. So for a class on visual storytelling with Tableau, I went looking for the rest of them and mapped what I found. (Some even have bathrooms.)
