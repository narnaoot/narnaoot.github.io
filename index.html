About

I'm a business analyst and data storyteller with 19+ years of experience. I spent seven years at Apple — collaborating with 40+ engineering teams, launching iCloud to 300M+ users, rebuilding kernel update workflows for 500K systems. Now I bring that same approach to San Francisco's transit network at SFMTA.

The through-line across every role: I translate complexity into clarity. I build the things that don't exist yet. I get teams unstuck.

I studied English Literature at the University of Chicago — that's where the storytelling instinct was born. An MS in Business Analytics with honors is where it got sharper.
